Soil

Good soil is dark and crumbly. Mix in compost every spring.

Test the soil with a small kit before planting.

Compost

  • Kitchen scraps
  • Dry leaves

Water

Water deeply twice a week, not a little each day.

Use mulch to keep the moisture in.

Schedule

  • Morning is best
  • Skip rainy days
